✨ Sanwar – Explained App Prompt
🧠 What is Sanwar?
Sanwar is a smart salon booking platform that helps local salon shopkeepers digitize their business and allows customers to easily discover and book services. Think of it like Zomato for salons, but with features inspired by IRCTC’s booking system — showing live available slots, clear pricing, and instant confirmations.

🎯 Target Users
Shopkeepers (Salon Owners) who want to:

Go digital

Avoid manual booking confusion

Look more professional

Attract more customers

Customers who want:

Quick, online salon bookings

No waiting lines

Trusted salons with reviews and prices

Instant confirmation

🌐 Landing Page (Homepage) Features
When someone visits the website, they will see:

✅ How many customers and salons are already using the platform

✅ What services are available (e.g., Haircut, Facial, Bridal, etc.)

✅ Live salon previews (images, ratings, top services)

✅ Call to Action buttons:

“Join as a Shopkeeper”

“Book a Salon Now”

✅ Explanation of how the platform works

🧑‍💼 Shopkeeper Features – What Can Salon Owners Do?
Create a Salon Profile

Add name, location (with map), photos, description

Add services with pricing and time (e.g., Haircut – ₹200 – 30 mins)

Add workers (name, photo, role)

Define Working Hours

Open/close time

Break/lunch time

Off days

Auto-Generate Time Slots

Based on the above timings and service durations

Like: 10:00–10:30, 10:30–11:00 etc.

Assigned to workers

These slots will automatically appear to customers

Set a Confirmation Amount

Shopkeeper can choose a small amount (e.g., ₹10, ₹20)

This amount is paid by the customer during booking

Ensures serious bookings only

Shows up clearly in customer flow (like: “Pay ₹20 to confirm”)

Booking Management Dashboard

See all bookings with:

Customer info

Selected service

Staff assigned

Paid status

Can also add walk-in customers manually

Ratings & Reviews

View and reply to customer reviews

Analytics Dashboard

See number of bookings

Peak hours

Top-performing services or staff

Income estimate

Optional Platform Fee (For Monetization)

Shopkeeper may need to pay a monthly fee (e.g., ₹100/month) to use the platform

Can be managed through the admin panel

🙋‍♂️ Customer Features – What Can Users Do?
Browse Salons

By location, rating, distance, or service

See each salon’s:

Photos

Available services and prices

Staff info

Time slots (with real-time availability like IRCTC)

Book a Time Slot

Select service, time, staff (optional)

If shopkeeper has set a confirmation amount, the customer must pay it instantly to confirm

Booking status and payment receipt are shown clearly

Manage Bookings

View upcoming or past bookings

Cancel or reschedule

Download receipts/invoices

Get SMS/Email/WhatsApp confirmations

Leave Ratings & Reviews

Rate the salon, service, and staff

Upload photos if they want

Referral Program

Invite friends and get wallet credit

Use credit to book next appointment
